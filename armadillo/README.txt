ARMADILLO - desktop pet that chases your cursor

BUILD THE EXE (needs Python from python.org, one time):
  1. Double-click build_exe.bat
  2. Wait a minute. Armadillo.exe appears in this folder.

OR just try it without building: double-click run_without_building.bat

CONTROLS
  Left click   pet it
  Right click  menu: nap, dig a hole, roll over here, size, quit

WHAT IT DOES
  Walks in side, front and back views, tilts its head up/down
  Curls into a ball and ROLLS when you are far away
  Naps (curled up, breathing, floating Zs) if you leave it alone, wakes with a "!"
  Idle: tail wag, blinking, sniffing, yawning, digging, looking around
  Size can be changed from the menu; command line: Armadillo.exe --scale 5
